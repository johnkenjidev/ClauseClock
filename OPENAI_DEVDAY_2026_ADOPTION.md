### Work / Codex
Prefer managed browser/code execution for document inspection, browser QA, and repository work instead of custom generic computer-use infrastructure.

### Space / Sites
May later be useful for collaborative review or dashboards, while canonical clause/deadline/evidence state remains in the product's governed store.

### Bounded decisions
Jev or any later verified bounded decision backend may classify:
- clause/topic relevance;
- evidence sufficiency;
- ambiguity/escalation;
- duplicate/related text.

It must not own:
- exact date arithmetic;
- legal conclusions;
- contract authority;
- money;
- permissions;
- external sends;
- publication/deployment.
